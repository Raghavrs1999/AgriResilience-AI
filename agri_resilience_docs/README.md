### Core Innovation Highlights (The "Unfair Advantage")

1. **Environmental Pathology Graph**: Fuses plant disease macro vision with live satellite radar telemetry (Sentinel-2 NDVI + ERA5 rainfall) to prevent rain wash-off disasters.
2. **True Multimodal Voice-First Accessibility**: Ingests spoken vernacular voice notes (Marathi, Bengali, Hindi, Bahasa Indonesia) directly into Gemini 1.5 Pro, eliminating the illiteracy barrier for rural smallholders.
3. **Hyperlocal Community Outbreak Radar**: Uses spatial geohash clustering to detect disease clusters in real time, alerting neighboring farmers within a 5km radius before spores spread.
4. **100% Google Cloud Native**: Leverages Cloud Run, Firebase Hosting, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Google Earth Engine to score maximum points under the 40% Technical Merit & Gen AI judging rubric.
